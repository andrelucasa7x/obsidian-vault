# 11 Camadas do Sistema

> Toda funcionalidade pertence a uma camada

---

| # | Camada | Descricao | Exemplo |
|---|--------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Captura** | RSS, scraping, scoring | news-curator, score-suggestions |
| 2 | **Geracao** | Texto, copy, roteiro | generate-content, agent-editor-chefe |
| 3 | **Midia** | Fotos, audios, videos | auto-improve-loop (busca imagem) |
| 4 | **Renderizacao** | Pixels finais | render-worker (Puppeteer → PNG) |
| 5 | **Avaliacao** | ML, editorial, A/B | quality-check (EfficientNet-B0) |
| 6 | **Publicacao** | Multi-canal | publish-post (IG, FB, WP, WhatsApp) |
| 7 | **Engajamento** | Bot, webhook, metricas | whatsapp-webhook, copiloto |
| 8 | **Aprendizado** | RLHF passivo, ML, prefs | taste-learner, agent_memory |
| 9 | **Inovacao** | R&D autonomo | P&D agents (T=0.9) |
| 10 | **Gestao** | CEO virtual, health, SEO | analytics-agent, dashboard |
| 11 | **Negocio** | Billing, affiliate, onboarding | Multi-tenant, onboarding futuro |
